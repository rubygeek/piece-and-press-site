The Story

Piece & Press is a sewing pattern business started with love for the craft. Every pattern is designed with care, tested thoroughly, and made for sewists who appreciate well-thought-out designs.

We believe sewing is a way to express yourself, create something unique, and slow down in a fast world.

Our Promise

  • Thoroughly tested patterns
  • Clear, easy-to-follow instructions
  • Thoughtful design with modern aesthetics
  • Inclusive sizing
